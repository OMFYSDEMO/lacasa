Our home

Not a hotel. A family house in the old heart of Thiruvananthapuram, with a room kept ready for you.

Lacasa is family-run and women-led. There's filter coffee in the morning, the smell of something cooking by noon, and someone at home to tell you which temple gate to use.

Morning

Filter coffee and slow mornings

Start the day the Kerala way — a steel tumbler of coffee, the newspaper, and the temple bells drifting over the rooftops.

Midday

A kitchen that feels like family

Spices, coconut and curry leaves — the kitchen is the heart of the house. Ask the hosts about home-style meals during your stay.

Evening

Everyone around one table

Back from the temple or the beach, families gather in the lounge, swap stories and settle in like they never left home.
